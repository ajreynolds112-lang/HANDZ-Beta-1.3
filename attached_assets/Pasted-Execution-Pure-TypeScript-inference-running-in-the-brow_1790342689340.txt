Action Heads & Action MaskingThe neural network outputs logits split into 3 distinct head outputs:String Head (250 Logits):Action Masking Rule: Before sampling/argmax, set logits of disallowed strings (due to stamina gates, difficulty lock, or role restrictions) to -Infinity. Softmax will evaluate masked strings to $0\%$ probability.Tempo Head (10 Bins):Log-spaced over 0.005s to 0.5s (e.g., 0.005, 0.01, 0.025, 0.05, 0.1, 0.18, 0.28, 0.38, 0.45, 0.50).Tactical Utility Head (6 Logits):0 = Standard Execution1 = Reset (B) (Triggers reset if available; speeds up rhythm for 1s)2 = Charge Arm (A) (Prepends Charge segment if stamina permits)3 = Slip-Left Counter (Initiates slip left, then executes string)4 = Slip-Right Counter (Initiates slip right, then executes string)5 = Stance Switch (Shift) (Prepends stance switch segment)C.
