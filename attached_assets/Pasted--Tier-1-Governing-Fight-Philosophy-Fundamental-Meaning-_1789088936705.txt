
Tier 1 — Governing Fight Philosophy
#	Fundamental	Meaning for the AI
1	Continuous Advantage (continuous_advantage)	Every action should gain position, information, initiative, stamina advantage, damage, or momentum. There is no truly neutral action. This combines your “gaining ground,” “small victories,” and momentum ideas.
2	Defensive Responsibility (defensive_responsibility)	Offense should never abandon defense, distance management, balance, or awareness of the return shot.
3	Decision Quality Under Pressure (pressure_decision_quality)	Winning ultimately means choosing the highest-value action while threatened, tired, hurt, or rushed.
4	Combat Composure (combat_composure)	Calmness improves perception, timing, patience, and opportunity recognition.
5	Constraint Control (constraint_control)	Control increases as the opponent loses viable options: movement, rhythm, timing, preferred punches, safe exits, etc.
6	Active Patience (active_patience)	Patience does not mean inactivity. Create reactions, gather information, threaten, and wait for favorable commitments.
7	Style Plasticity (style_plasticity)	The AI should be able to change style rather than remain permanently aggressive, defensive, counter-oriented, or evasive.
8	Layered Adaptation (layered_adaptation)	Continuously make small adjustments, and when the opponent adapts, answer with multiple interacting adaptations rather than one predictable response.
Tier 2 — Fight Intelligence & Prediction
#	Fundamental	Meaning for the AI
9	Information Harvesting (information_harvesting)	Early actions should reveal range, reactions, habits, defensive preferences, counters, and tendencies.
10	Predictive Timing (predictive_timing)	Timing is pattern recognition plus prediction: determine when the opponent is likely to commit, move, punch, defend, or reset. This absorbs “timing beats speed.”
11	Habit Exploitation (habit_exploitation)	Detect repeated behaviors, bad habits, predictable counters, and overcommitments and construct punishments for them.
12	Threat Assumption (threat_assumption)	Behave as though the opponent is always attempting to set up a counter or trap until evidence says otherwise.
13	Impact Diagnosis (impact_diagnosis)	After getting hit, identify the cause: range error, timing error, careless offense, wrong defense, bad defensive timing, etc., then alter behavior.
14	Defense Vulnerability Modeling (defense_vulnerability_model)	Every defensive system exposes something. Identify what the opponent's chosen defense gives up.
15	Counter-Threat Mapping (counter_threat_mapping)	Predict which counter is most dangerous given your intended punch, position, distance, and opponent tendencies, then select defense accordingly.
16	Risk-Reward Counter Selection (counter_expected_value)	Prefer counters that maximize damage and positional gain relative to the danger accepted.
